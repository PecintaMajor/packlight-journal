Compact Travel Kettles: A 2026 Roundup

Notes from the road, November 2026. A travel kettle earns its place in a bag only if it is light, quick and hard to break. We carried four of them through a month of trains and hostels. Two are covered in depth below; the others are summarised at the end.

TrailMate Mini

The Mini is the budget pick: a simple single-wall kettle that boils fast and shrugs off dents. It has no insulation to speak of, so a second cup means a second boil.

KampaLite 0.8

A folding silicone design that packs almost flat. Clever, but the hinge near the spout weeps a little when poured at an angle.

The rest, briefly

The NordBoil Go is sturdy and slow. A fourth kettle, sold under several names, stopped working in week two and is not recommended.

Our verdict: pack the kettle whose compromises match your trip. For most rail journeys this year, we reached for the insulated ones.
